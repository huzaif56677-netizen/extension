# Privacy Policy for NotesSmith

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

NotesSmith is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how NotesSmith handles user data.

## 1. No Data Collection
NotesSmith does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or user data. 

## 2. Local Storage Only
- All notes, titles, text formatting, and attached images are stored strictly on your local device using standard browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- No data is ever sent to external servers, cloud storage, analytics services, or third parties.

## 3. Permissions Usage
NotesSmith requests only the minimal permissions necessary to operate:
- **`storage` / `unlimitedStorage`**: To save your created notes and attached images locally in your browser.
- **`activeTab`**: To allow you to take screenshots of the current tab when you click the "Screenshot" button, and to display the companion pet on the active tab when summoned.
- **`scripting`**: To dynamically attach the companion pet to existing web pages when you click "Show Pet".
- **`<all_urls>`**: To allow the companion pet and notes features to function on any webpage you choose to summon it on.

## 4. Contact
For any questions or support regarding NotesSmith, please open an issue on the project repository.
